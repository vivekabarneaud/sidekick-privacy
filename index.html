Privacy Policy

Sidekick · last updated 27 November 2026

Sidekick is an app to record the small exercises that you do during the day. It shows your totals and your progress. It can also show your steps and your runs.

What Sidekick collects

Nothing. Sidekick has no account and no sign-in. It has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reports, and no trackers.

Sidekick keeps your exercises and your logs in its private storage on your phone. Sidekick has no server. It does not send your data anywhere, and we do not receive your data.

Steps

Sidekick counts your steps with the step sensor of your phone, but only if you push "Count my steps" and give the permission "Physical activity". Sidekick saves the value of the sensor on your phone, approximately every 15 minutes, to calculate your steps for each day and each week. Sidekick does not send this data anywhere.

You can stop the count at any time. Open the settings of your phone, select Sidekick, and remove the permission "Physical activity".

Runs from Health Connect

Health Connect is the store for health data on Android. A watch or an app (for example, Samsung Health) puts data there. If you give the permission, Sidekick reads one type of data from Health Connect:

  • Exercise: to show the number and the duration of your runs this week. Sidekick uses only the sessions of the type "running".

Sidekick only reads this data. It does not write data to Health Connect, and it does not keep a copy. Sidekick shows the data on your phone and does not send it to any person, server, or company. Sidekick does not use the data for advertising, and it does not sell or share the data.

You can remove the permission at any time. Open Health Connect on your phone and select Sidekick. The other parts of Sidekick continue to work without the permission.

The use of the data from Health Connect obeys the Google Play requirements for Health Connect, which include the Limited Use requirements.

Android's own backup

By default, Android makes a backup of the private data of apps, also of Sidekick. Android can copy this data to your own Google account. It can then put the data back on a new phone. This backup does not include the data in Health Connect.

This backup is a part of Android, not of Sidekick. We do not receive the data and we cannot get access to it. You can stop it for all apps in the settings of your phone, under Google → Backup.

Delete your data

When you remove Sidekick from your phone, Android deletes all the data of Sidekick. The data in Health Connect stays in Health Connect, because it does not belong to Sidekick. We keep no data, so you do not need to ask us to delete data.

Children

Sidekick collects no personal information from any person, also from children.
