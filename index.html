Privacy Policy

Sidekick · last updated 28 November 2026

Sidekick is an app to record the small exercises that you do during the day. It shows your totals and your progress. It can also show your steps and your runs.

What Sidekick collects

Nothing. Sidekick has no account and no sign-in. It has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reports, and no trackers.

Sidekick keeps your exercises and your logs in its private storage on your phone. Sidekick has no server. It does not send your data anywhere, and we do not receive your data.

Steps and runs

Sidekick counts your steps and finds your runs, but only if you push "Count my steps and runs" and give the permission "Physical activity".

  • Steps: Sidekick reads the step sensor of your phone when you open it, and approximately every 15 minutes in the background. It saves the values on your phone, to calculate your steps for each day and each week.
  • Runs: the activity recognition of Google Play services on your phone tells Sidekick when you start and stop to run. Sidekick saves only these two times, to show the number and the duration of your runs. Google Play services is a part of Android, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to it.

Sidekick does not send this data to any person, server, or company. It does not use the data for advertising, and it does not sell or share it.

You can stop the count at any time. Open the settings of your phone, select Sidekick, and remove the permission "Physical activity". The other parts of Sidekick continue to work without the permission.

Updates

When Sidekick starts, it asks the update server of Expo (the company that makes the tools for Sidekick) if a new version exists. This request contains the version of the app and the type of phone, but none of your data.

Backups that you make

If you select a backup folder, Sidekick writes a copy of your data there as a file. You select the folder, and only you decide where the file goes. Sidekick does not send the file anywhere.

Android's own backup

By default, Android makes a backup of the private data of apps, also of Sidekick. Android can copy this data to your own Google account. It can then put the data back on a new phone.

This backup is a part of Android, not of Sidekick. We do not receive the data and we cannot get access to it. You can stop it for all apps in the settings of your phone, under Google → Backup.

Delete your data

When you remove Sidekick from your phone, Android deletes all the data of Sidekick. We keep no data, so you do not need to ask us to delete data.

Children

Sidekick collects no personal information from any person, also from children.
